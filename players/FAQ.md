# Frequently Asked Questions

# Why can't I get a farm?

Check that you have permission for `/farm get`, that the farm type exists, and that you can afford the cost.

If you did not enter a farm type, the server needs a default farm type configured.

# Why can't I place my farm?

Common reasons are disabled worlds, claim requirements, missing claim trust, farm overlap, water or lava at the placement area, owner-locked farm items, or farm limits.

The error message usually names the exact reason.

# Why does my farm say it has no blocks?

Place harvestable blocks inside the farm radius. Use the **Harvestable Blocks** page in the farm menu to see what this farm type accepts.

For crop farms, place configured crops. For tree farms, place configured saplings or propagules.

# Why did my farm stop growing?

Open the farm menu and check the status item.

Most stopped farms need fuel, empty storage, loaded chunks, harvestable blocks, the owner online, or a manual resume.

# Why are items not going into my inventory automatically?

Harvested items are stored inside the farm storage. Right-click the farm to open storage and collect them.

If your server uses UpgradeableHoppers, an upgradeable hopper placed directly under the farm can transport items automatically.

# Why can't I edit or remove a farm?

You need the correct farm role. The owner can trust you, change your role, or remove you from the farm.

Server staff may also have bypass permissions, but normal players are controlled by the farm's trusted-player roles.

# What should I do before removing a farm?

Collect the items from farm storage first. Removing a valid loaded farm gives the farm item back, but storage contents are not the same thing as the farm item.

# Why does growth look slow or unusual?

Growth timing, visual growth stages, harvest drops, and accepted blocks are configured by the server. Some servers also delay visual updates for performance, which does not necessarily mean the farm is producing slower.
