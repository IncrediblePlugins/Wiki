# Relations

Relations decide whether another land or nation is neutral, allied, or an enemy.

# Allies

Allies are friendly lands or nations. Depending on your server settings, allies may be able to help in wars, share some access, or appear with ally colors in menus and nametags.

To send an ally request from your current edit land:

`/lands relations allies add <land or nation>`

To remove an ally:

`/lands relations allies remove <land or nation>`

The other side may need to accept an ally request before the alliance becomes active.

# Enemies

Enemies are hostile lands or nations.

Some servers require you to mark a land or nation as an enemy before declaring war on it.

To mark another land or nation as an enemy from your current edit land:

`/lands relations enemies add <land or nation>`

To request peace or remove the enemy relation:

`/lands relations enemies remove <land or nation>`

# Nation Relations

If your land is part of a nation, the nation may manage relations for its member lands.

Use:

`/nations relations`

The command works like land relations:

* `/nations relations allies add <land or nation>`
* `/nations relations allies remove <land or nation>`
* `/nations relations enemies add <land or nation>`
* `/nations relations enemies remove <land or nation>`

# Menu

You can also manage relations through `/lands relations`, `/nations relations`, or the relations button in the land or nation menu.
