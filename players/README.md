# ChestProtect Player Wiki

This section explains ChestProtect from a player's point of view.

ChestProtect lets you lock containers, doors, hoppers, item frames, armor stands, and other objects
that your server has enabled. Use these pages when you want to protect your items, share a
protection with trusted players, manage roles, or adjust your personal settings.
