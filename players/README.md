# BetterFarming Player Guide

This section explains BetterFarming from a player's point of view.

BetterFarming lets you place special farm items in the world. A placed farm manages the blocks around it, grows configured crops or saplings on a timer, harvests them into internal storage, and can be upgraded if your server allows it.

Servers can change farm types, costs, fuel items, limits, protected worlds, roles, and accepted harvest blocks. The pages here describe the default gameplay flow and call out where your server's setup may be different.
