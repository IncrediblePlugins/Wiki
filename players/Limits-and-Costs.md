# Limits and Costs

Servers can limit how many protections you may own and how much locking costs.

Your limits and costs depend on the server configuration, your permission group, playtime rewards,
and any admin-set limit changes.

# Protection Limits

The main menu shows how many block and entity protections you own.

Common limits are:

| Limit | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Block protections | How many blocks you can lock, such as chests, barrels, doors, hoppers, and shulker boxes. |
| Entity protections | How many entities you can lock, such as armor stands, item frames, or enabled animals. |
| Groups | How many trust groups you can create. |
| Protection members | How many players can be trusted to one protection or group. |

If you reach a limit, unlock unused protections or ask staff how limits work on your server.

# Free Protections

Servers can give players a number of free block or entity protections.

After you use your free amount, locking may cost money or item currency.

# Lock Costs

Each protectable type can have its own cost. For example, a server can make hoppers cheaper than
shulker boxes or make armor stands more expensive than containers.

If your server uses a global lock cost, every protectable object uses the same cost instead.

Auto lock uses the same cost and limit checks as `/chestprotect lock`.

# Cashback

Servers can return part of the lock cost when you unlock a protection.

Cashback depends on the protectable type and server configuration. Protections managed by another
plugin may not pay cashback through ChestProtect.

# Teleport Costs

When you open your protection list from `/chestprotect`, your server may allow teleporting to a
protection. Teleporting can have a wait time and a cost.

The permission `chestprotect.teleport.protection` controls whether you may teleport to protections.
